DIVI Connect

Sign in and out of work from your phone.

OPEN DIVI CONNECT

Put it on your iPhone home screen

  1. Make sure this page is open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. From now on, tap the DIVI Connect icon to sign in and out.

Put it on your Android home screen

  1. Make sure this page is open in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.
  4. From now on, tap the DIVI Connect icon to sign in and out.
